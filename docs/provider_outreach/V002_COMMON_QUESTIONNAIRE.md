# V002 Provider Written-Confirmation Questionnaire

Status: review draft only; do not send without separate human approval

Please answer **yes**, **no**, or **not applicable**, then cite the contract,
schema, file specification, or written certification supporting each answer.
Marketing pages alone will not be treated as capability evidence.

1. Are participant/exchange timestamps and SIP/provider-receipt timestamps both retained? State precision and timezone.
2. Are sequence identifiers complete per symbol, tape, channel, and session? How are gaps proven or classified?
3. Can every correction and cancellation be reconstructed without overwriting the original record?
4. Are raw historical files immutable? If revised, are all vintages, publication times, and supersession links available?
5. Are historical reference, action, status, and content revisions versioned point in time?
6. Can bounded negative coverage be established for every requested security and interval, rather than inferred from an empty result?
7. Do delisted, renamed, transferred, merged, and recycled-symbol securities remain represented with stable identifiers?
8. Is symbol lineage point-in-time, with effective and first-known timestamps?
9. Can complete daily universe snapshots be reconstructed as known before 09:25 America/New_York without survivorship bias?
10. Does the license permit local immutable storage, hashing, derived normalized records, and internal quantitative research?
11. What are the exact historical start/end coverage, update cadence, and retained correction history?
12. Are bulk flat files available? Provide schemas, checksums, file identity, pagination, and completeness guarantees.
13. What is the estimated archive size for 2024-05-03 through 2025-06-04?
14. State one-time charges, recurring price, exchange/license fees, rate limits, download limits, and redownload terms.
15. Is written certification or contractual confirmation of these answers available?
16. Are raw payloads available under the same retention and research-use rights?
17. How are source outages, late files, conflicting records, and corrected files disclosed?
18. Can a sample schema and a non-empirical metadata-only file inventory be reviewed before purchase?

No sample containing empirical rows should be supplied during this outreach
phase. Schema and metadata-only evidence are sufficient for this questionnaire.
