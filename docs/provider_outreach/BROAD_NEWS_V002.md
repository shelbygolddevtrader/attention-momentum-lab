# Broad News and Catalyst Provider Questionnaire

Status: unsent review draft; provider not selected

The common questionnaire in `V002_COMMON_QUESTIONNAIRE.md` is incorporated in
full. A broad-news provider should additionally confirm:

1. Does the archive cover every V002 eligible security and required catalyst category, including analyst, offering, FDA/clinical, legal/enforcement, M&A, rumor, and issuer disclosures?
2. Are original publication, provider receipt, update, correction, retraction, retrieval, and filing-acceptance timestamps retained where applicable?
3. Are syndicated copies, canonical documents, and every raw story separately immutable and linkable?
4. Can category-level and security-window negative coverage be proven rather than inferred from empty searches?
5. Quote the historical archive license, local raw-payload retention, internal quantitative-research rights, delivery format, size, and correction history.
