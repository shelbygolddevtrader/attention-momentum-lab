# Professional Strategy Divisions and Capital Boundary V001

The Benchmark Division contains only the ten preregistered, widely used
professional day-trading strategy families. It establishes transparent simple
comparators; it does not assert that they are the objectively top ten. A
discovered archetype cannot enter this division after outcomes are known.

The Research Division is reserved for separately approved attention-momentum
versions, Winner Archetype candidates, future discovery-generated strategies,
and ensembles. It is empty in V001. A future entry needs its own frozen contract
and hypothesis-family treatment before protected outcomes are opened.

The exhibition classification is for a contract that lacks sufficient evidence
or cannot be represented honestly by the available dataset. Exhibition results,
if a later protocol permits them, are clearly separated, receive no medal, do
not affect official ranks, and earn no advancement credit. Short contracts stay
here unless point-in-time locates and borrow fees are proven.

## Capital boundary

Olympics ranking can nominate an unchanged strategy for the next research gate;
it cannot grant that gate. Every candidate must still separately pass discovery,
untouched validation, one-time holdout, prospective paper-forward, tiny-live,
self-funding, and controlled-scaling gates with required human approvals.

No medal authorizes paper or live operation, position-size increases, capital
release, platform funding, provider purchases, or production deployment. The
capital-governance identity is bound into the Olympics protocol and cannot be
substituted. Only settled realized net profits can later fund the separate
data/platform reserve under that governance contract; backtest PnL, paper PnL,
unrealized PnL, deposits, and forecasts cannot.

All V001 authorizations are false. This document creates no broker interface and
no authority to acquire data, run a pilot, inspect validation or holdout, or
transfer capital.
